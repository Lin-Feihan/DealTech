## 1. Executive Thesis

Soren’s most credible first-wave opportunity set remains **sponsor-backed provider-services and specialty behavioral-health platforms** where operating scale, reimbursement exposure, consolidation logic, and public-market messaging can be diligenced with discipline rather than guessed at.

Within that frame, **physician-services / MSO platforms still screen best**, particularly when they combine visible operating footprint, sponsor ownership, and a believable use for public equity currency. Behavioral-health platforms remain highly relevant, but they demand a tighter underwriting standard around billing quality, staffing durability, outcomes support, and disclosure readiness.

The current screen identifies **3 names for active validation**:

1. **US Heart and Vascular**
2. **Centria Healthcare / Centria Autism**
3. **Hopebridge**

The remaining shortlisted names are still strategically relevant, but they should not be treated as equivalent outreach priorities until route timing, scale visibility, and public-company readiness become more concrete.

This ranking should be read as **current validation priority**, not a final transaction recommendation. Public evidence supports the broad strategic direction, but current public sources do not establish seller intent, PIPE receptivity, management rollover, or audit readiness with the level of confidence required for sponsor engagement.
