# Limitations and Human Review

These agents do not provide investment, legal, tax, regulatory, valuation, or fairness advice. They produce structured research outputs and certification status for further professional review.

Human review is mandatory when:

- source evidence is weak or secondary-only;
- the evidence comes from imported artifacts or old demo outputs;
- material calculations are not replayed from primary data;
- legal, regulatory, compliance, or transaction-readiness conclusions are asserted;
- the case relies on unavailable private-company financials or undisclosed transaction terms.
