# PCE Certification Gate

PCE is the certification boundary. It does not make investment recommendations and does not simply trust an existing `pce_audit_current_run.csv`.

On each demo run, PCE rebuilds certification rows by cross-checking:

- `hard_filter_table.csv`
- `er_brb_scoring_table.csv`
- `dd_evidence_table.csv`
- `risk_matrix.csv`
- `financial_calculation_sheet.csv`
- `claim_to_evidence_map.csv`

## Allowed Statuses

PCE uses only these statuses:

- `Certified`
- `Certified with Caveat`
- `Internal Trace Only`
- `Needs Human Review`
- `Not Certified`

## Downgrade Rules

- Missing evidence becomes `Not Certified` or `Needs Human Review` depending on scope.
- `needs_review`, metadata-only, title-level, or unverified PDF evidence cannot become unconditional external `Certified`.
- Upstream `human_review_required = Yes` propagates to claim-level PCE.
- Calculations that cannot be replayed are downgraded to `Certified with Caveat` or `Needs Human Review`.
- Risk claims without adequate risk-matrix mapping are kept caveated or internal-only.

## Current Tuntun HK Demo Status

The current example intentionally produces a conservative, scoped result:

- `certification_status`: `Certified with Caveat`
- `final_delivery_allowed`: `true`

This does **not** mean the repository, the whole trace, or all claims are fully certified. It means the scoped external-final delivery gate allows only the subset of final-delivery material `CLM-*` claims that:

1. are explicitly referenced in the delivery files;
2. exist in `trace/claim_to_evidence_map.csv`;
3. exist in `pce_audit/pce_audit_current_run.csv`;
4. have `delivery_scope = external_final`; and
5. have PCE status `Certified` or `Certified with Caveat`.

In the current mapped delivery, the referenced external-final claims are `Certified`. No `Needs Human Review`, `Internal Trace Only`, or `Not Certified` claim is allowed into external-final delivery unless it is clearly described as an internal limitation rather than a final external conclusion.

Important caveats remain:

- large numbers of trace rows still remain `Needs Human Review` or `Internal Trace Only`;
- most trace artifacts are `imported_from_original_bundle` + `pipeline_validated`, not fully regenerated by the current pipeline;
- content not covered by explicit external-final `CLM-*` claim mapping cannot be used as a final external conclusion;
- the status must not be described as `Fully Certified` or `All Claims Certified`.
