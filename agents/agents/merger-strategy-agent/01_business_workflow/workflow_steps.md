# Workflow Steps — Merger Strategy Agent

The workflow below is based on the user-provided merger strategy framework image.

## 0. Intent and clarification

- Capture buyer, target, transaction context, geography, sector, timeframe, and user objective.
- Ask clarifying questions when core inputs are missing.
- Do not proceed to certification until a real case input and source registry exist.

## 1. Transaction Overview
- Parties involved; transaction timing; why this deal; why this buyer / why this target.

## 2. Standalone Assessment
- Buyer standalone business model; target standalone business model; baseline case before merger effects.

## 3. Stakeholder Analysis
- Shareholders, management, board, employees, customers, regulators, lenders, and other material stakeholders.

## 4. Industry & Market Analysis
- Industry trends, competitive dynamics, growth, cyclicality, pricing, regulation, and disruption.

## 5. Valuation
- DCF / projection model; trading comparables; transaction precedents; adjusted valuation cases; walkaway price.

## 6. Synergies & Value Creation
- Revenue synergies; cost synergies; financial synergies; strategic value creation; realization timing and execution risk.

## 7. Deal Structure & Financing
- Cash / stock / debt mix; tax; financing availability; leverage, covenants, and balance-sheet capacity.

## 8. Pro Forma Financial Impact
- Pro forma P&L; balance sheet; free cash flow; ROIC / WACC; accretion / dilution.

## 9. Governance & Control
- Board composition; voting/control terms; covenants and consent rights; post-merger governance model.

## 10. Deal Diligence Findings
- Financial, legal, tax, commercial, and operational diligence.

## 11. Regulatory & Antitrust Risk
- Relevant jurisdictions; filing requirements; antitrust risk; remedies, timing, and approval uncertainty.

## 12. Integration Plan
- Day-1 plan; 100-day plan; operating model; integration governance; technology, people, and process integration.

## 13. Risk Analysis
- Key transaction risks, mitigation plans, execution risk, market risk, reputational and stakeholder risk.

## 14. Scenario & Sensitivity Analysis
- Bull case, base case, bear/downside case, worst case, key sensitivity drivers.

## 15. Final Recommendation
- Strategic rationale, financial attractiveness, risk-adjusted decision, go / no-go / renegotiate recommendation, caveats and human-review blockers.

## Tool-use layers

- **Offline analysis:** financial model, DCF/projection, valuation, scenario and sensitivity analysis.
- **Online retrieval:** SEC filings, company corpus, industry data, market data, pricing data, news, equity research.
- **Extended tool use:** API search, browser retrieval, precedent transactions, contracts, reports, and other validated corpora.
