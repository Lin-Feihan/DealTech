# ER/BRB Framework — Merger Strategy Agent

ER/BRB must evaluate:

- Source reliability.
- Business risk.
- Regulatory / antitrust risk.
- Reputational and stakeholder risk.
- Evidence sufficiency.
- Calculation replay sufficiency.
- Human review requirement.
- Certification status.

High-risk merger claims, especially valuation, synergies, pro forma financial impact, regulatory/antitrust risk, fairness, and final recommendation, require human review unless primary evidence and calculation replay are complete.
