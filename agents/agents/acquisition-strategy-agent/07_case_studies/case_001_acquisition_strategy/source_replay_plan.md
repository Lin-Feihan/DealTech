# Source Replay Plan — Apple → DarwinAI Acquisition Strategy

## Known facts to replay

- Buyer identity: Apple Inc.
- Target identity: DarwinAI.
- Transaction type: private-target acquisition / acqui-hire style public reporting.
- Public factual background: Apple has an AI strategy and large financial capacity; DarwinAI is associated with efficient / explainable AI technology; public reporting says Apple acquired DarwinAI.

## Unknown or non-public facts

- DarwinAI revenue, EBITDA, cash balance, backlog, customer contracts, and audited financials.
- Deal value, consideration mix, closing mechanics, retention economics, earn-outs, indemnities, or valuation multiple.
- Fairness opinion, board materials, shareholder consent, alternative-buyer process, or formal go/no-go recommendation.

## Acceptable source types

- Apple SEC filings and official Apple disclosures for Apple identity, scale, and financial-capacity context.
- Target official website/profile or archived official company materials for target identity and technical description.
- Reputable transaction reporting for public transaction occurrence, with caveat when Apple/target did not publish terms.
- Replayed calculation workpapers for valuation, multiples, premium, EPS, synergy, or fairness claims.

## Non-PCE-eligible source types

- Imported demo/report artifacts.
- LLM summaries.
- Scenario models without source replay.
- Secondary summaries used as the only support for high-risk valuation, fairness, or recommendation claims.
- Any source row marked `source replay pending` or `calculation not replayed`.

## PCE certification rule

Factual background may be Certified or Certified with Caveat when a registered source and evidence row exist. Strategic rationale may be Certified with Caveat when it is labeled as inference and evidence-supported. Valuation, pricing, fairness, board recommendation, go/no-go, accept/reject/negotiate, or investment/legal advice claims are Human Review Required or Not Certified unless source replay and calculation replay are both complete.
