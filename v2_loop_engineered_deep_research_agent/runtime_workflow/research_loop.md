# Research Loop

## Purpose

The research loop executes one module of the research plan at a time and writes structured outputs into the evidence repository and claim-evidence graph.

## Inputs

- mandate
- research plan
- active module
- prior research gaps
- source quality policy
- available internal materials

## Actions

1. Convert active module into specific research questions.
2. Identify preferred source types.
3. Search, retrieve, or inspect sources.
4. Extract relevant evidence.
5. Create candidate claims.
6. Create calculation records when numeric outputs are produced.
7. Link claims to evidence and sources.
8. Record unresolved questions as research gaps.

## Outputs

- evidence items
- source records
- candidate claims
- calculation records
- updated claim-evidence graph
- research gaps

## Non-Goals

The research loop should not produce final recommendations directly. It may propose candidate analytical claims, but those must pass certification.

## Return Conditions

Return to research when certification identifies:

- missing source support
- insufficient evidence
- unreliable source
- failed calculation replay
- unresolved conflict
- missing module coverage
- human-review item that can be reduced by more research

## Buyer-Side Acquisition Modules

The first V2 pilot uses the 14 buyer-side acquisition modules from the V1 prompt/spec. Each module should be independently researchable and certifiable.
