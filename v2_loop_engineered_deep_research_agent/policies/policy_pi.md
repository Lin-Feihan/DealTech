# Policy π

## Purpose

Policy π is the V2 claim-level certification policy. It decides whether a generated claim can enter downstream deal analysis or final report output.

The principle is inherited from the V1 Certified DealTech work:

```text
Generation does not equal permission.
```

A claim may be generated by a research agent, but it is not deliverable until it is certified, caveated, escalated, or blocked.

## Inputs

Policy π evaluates:

- claim statement
- claim type
- linked evidence
- linked sources
- source reliability
- calculation records
- dependencies on other claims
- conflicts with other claims
- human-review flags
- report impact

## Certification Status

### Certified

The claim is sufficiently supported and may enter final analysis/reporting.

Requirements:

- material sources are identified
- evidence directly supports the claim
- source reliability is acceptable
- calculations are replayable when material\n- no unresolved material conflict exists
- no human-review condition blocks use

### Certified with Caveat

The claim may enter final analysis/reporting only with visible limitations.

Use when:

- evidence is sufficient but dated
- source reliability is acceptable but not ideal
- calculation depends on disclosed assumptions
- conflicting sources exist but are disclosed and resolved directionally
- the claim is directionally useful but not fully definitive

### Needs Human Review

The claim cannot support a final recommendation without human review.

Use when:

- legal, regulatory, tax, accounting, or valuation judgment exceeds automated confidence
- material source conflict remains unresolved
- user-provided data conflicts with source-backed data
- strategic judgment depends on unverifiable assumptions
- management intent, board position, or negotiation stance is inferred rather than documented

### Not Certified

The claim must not enter final analysis/reporting.

Use when:

- no supporting evidence exists
- evidence does not support the statement
- source is unreliable for the claim type
- calculation fails replay
- material conflict invalidates the claim
- claim is speculative but phrased as fact

### Internal Trace Only

The claim is retained for audit, gap tracking, or internal reasoning, but must not appear as a reportable conclusion.

Use when:

- the item is an upstream screening trace
- the item is a risk signal not yet verified
- the item explains why another research path was taken
- the item is relevant to process but not final output

## Claim Type Requirements

### Factual Claim

Requires direct source-backed evidence.

### Financial Claim

Requires source date, unit, currency, and period.

### Calculation Claim

Requires formula, inputs, units, and replayable calculation record.

### Valuation Claim

Requires method, date, assumptions, sensitivity, and explicit caveats.

### Risk Claim

Requires evidence or clearly labeled risk rationale. Unsupported risk signals should be caveated or kept internal.

### Strategic Judgment Claim

Requires dependency links to certified factual, financial, market, risk, and valuation claims.

### Recommendation Claim

Requires certified or caveated upstream support. If the recommendation depends on Needs Human Review items, the recommendation must be restricted or escalated.

## Output Rule

A final report may include:

- Certified claims
- Certified with Caveat claims, with visible caveats

A final report must not rely on:

- Not Certified claims
- Internal Trace Only claims
- Needs Human Review claims unless the report is explicitly restricted and labeled as requiring human review
